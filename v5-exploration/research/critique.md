## A — Architecture Ledger

Strengths: immediate corporate credibility, restrained typography, excellent maturity hierarchy, and the easiest GitHub-safe translation.

Weaknesses: can feel like a polished résumé document; project architecture and AI proof are subordinate rather than memorable.

## B — Control Plane

Strengths: strongest platform/delivery topology outside the finalists; engineering breadth is unmistakable and relationships are explicit.

Weaknesses: dense on mobile and closest to a monitoring/dashboard aesthetic, which risks reintroducing a milder form of the V4 problem.

## C — Capability Atlas

Strengths: best explicit distinction between established depth, applied work, platform context, and expansion; visually original without neon.

Weaknesses: the spatial map costs scanning speed and becomes a long vertical composition on mobile; project decisions are less dominant.

## D — Architecture Dossiers

Strengths: deepest project proof, excellent recruiter-to-engineer progression, memorable technical-editorial character, and exceptionally clear failure-model framing.

Weaknesses: slower ten-second scan than F; text-rich case plates require careful SVG typography and disciplined mobile splitting.

Refinement: reduced decorative styling, strengthened case-study rhythm, retained a restrained serif/sans editorial contrast, and recomposed mobile cases vertically.

## E — Assurance Loop

Strengths: most credible AI engineering story, excellent authority boundaries, and the strongest Software Factory representation.

Weaknesses: can over-weight AI/control systems and understate frontend depth if adopted alone; animated workflow translation is the most complex finalist implementation.

Refinement: brought React/TypeScript back into the primary identity, simplified the control-plane geometry, and kept unimplemented capabilities in an explicit boundary section.

## F — System Brief

Strengths: best balance of recruiter speed and engineering depth; frontend remains primary while systems, projects, delivery, and AI read as one coherent practice.

Weaknesses: the architecture map will become generic if production adds every candidate technology; it depends on ruthless curation.

Refinement: removed an unsupported foreground WebSocket implication, improved the semantic request-path motion, tightened capability hierarchy, and increased mobile contrast and spacing.

## Finalists

1. F — System Brief
2. D — Architecture Dossiers
3. E — Assurance Loop
