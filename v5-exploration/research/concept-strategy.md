# V5 Concept Strategy

## A — Architecture Ledger

Enterprise engineering documentation as identity. A horizontal responsibility architecture, evidence ledger, and governed delivery loop. Best for corporate clarity and fast scanning.

## B — Control Plane

Systems topology and delivery relationships. The profile reads as a technical operations map without adopting a HUD aesthetic. Best for platform breadth and runtime awareness.

## C — Capability Atlas

A hierarchy map with axes for product-to-runtime reach and established-to-expanding maturity. Best at preventing a broad stack from reading as unfocused or inflated.

## D — Architecture Dossiers

Projects dominate as technical editorial case studies. Decisions, constraints, and proof are more important than technology names. Best for engineering managers who will read deeply.

## E — Assurance Loop

AI engineering is the central system: decision, execution, and evidence planes; deterministic verification; independent review; human authority. Best proof of serious agentic-engineering maturity.

## F — System Brief

An executive technical brief combining a recruiter-fast responsibility map, two memorable architecture decisions, and the Software Factory assurance system. Designed as the strongest balance of clarity, depth, originality, and GitHub feasibility.
