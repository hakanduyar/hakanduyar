ENGINEERING POSITIONING:
Frontend-first systems engineer: React and TypeScript product depth, application architecture, service/data boundaries, delivery awareness, and evidence-governed AI-assisted engineering.

PRIMARY STACK:
React, TypeScript, JavaScript, Next.js; state, query, forms, accessible UI, PWA/local-first architecture, and API/data integration.

BROADER ENGINEERING LANDSCAPE:
Applied Node.js, Express/NestJS, REST, JWT/RBAC, PostgreSQL, Prisma, Supabase, IndexedDB/Dexie; Git/CI, Docker, Nginx, Linux, and deployment context. Kubernetes, Ansible, Grafana, and K9s are clearly separated as expansion/operational context.

AI ENGINEERING POSITIONING:
AI as a governed engineering participant across analysis, planning, implementation, deterministic verification, independent review, bounded repair, and human release authority. Software Factory is the primary public proof.

ARCHITECTURE STRATEGY:
Make responsibility, boundary, data flow, failure model, delivery path, and evidence lineage the visual narrative. Technologies appear where they perform a system role, never as a badge wall.
